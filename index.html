Hospital & clinic cash prices vs. a fair price = 80% of Medicare

Important: These are estimates compiled from hospitals' public machine-readable price files and providers' websites. They are not quotes and not medical, legal or financial advice. Hospital file prices are usually the facility fee only; physician, anesthesia, radiologist and pathology (professional) fees are typically billed separately and are estimated here at 80% of Medicare where missing. Prices change; always confirm the cash price in writing with the provider before care. Each row links to its source price page / file.
