Fair Cash Price

Hospital & clinic cash prices vs. an implied fair price = your chosen % of Medicare

Important: These are estimates compiled from hospitals' public machine-readable price files and providers' websites. They are not quotes and not medical, legal or financial advice. Hospital file prices are usually the facility fee only; physician, anesthesia, radiologist and pathology (professional) fees are typically billed separately and are estimated here at your chosen reimbursement rate (default 75%) of Medicare where missing. Prices change; always confirm the cash price in writing with the provider before care. Each row links to its source price page / file.
